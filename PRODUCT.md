# CampusFlow — Product Manifesto

## Vision & Charter
CampusFlow (CampusPro) is an enterprise-grade digital super-app designed for university campuses across Ethiopia, piloted at **Haramaya University Main Campus (Bate)** before expanding nationwide. It consolidates essential student life—cafeteria food pre-orders, Dire Dawa/Harar bus ticketing, off-campus accommodation, campus events, and academic past papers—into a unified platform.

## Target Personas
1. **The Campus Student:** Needs fast, zero-slop mobile & web access to cafeteria orders between lectures, bus seat reservations home on weekends, and study materials without burning expensive mobile data.
2. **The Campus Merchant / Cafe Owner:** Operates under low-cost constraints; needs clear audible order chimes, real-time item toggling, and clean transaction tracking.
3. **The Transport Operator:** Shuttles students between Haramaya, Harar, and Dire Dawa with QR ticket verification and seat layout management.
4. **Campus Administration & Landlords:** Off-campus student housing management and verified event credentials.

## Guiding Principles & Constraints
- **Zero Floating-Point Financials:** All money is stored in integer minor units (`BigInt` in Ethiopian Birr cents).
- **Offline & Low-Bandwidth Resilience:** Optimized bundle sizes, fast initial paint, and crisp SVG icons.
- **Sunlight Readability:** High-contrast text palettes suitable for outdoor mobile use under bright sunlight.
- **Legal Compliance:** Compliant with Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.
